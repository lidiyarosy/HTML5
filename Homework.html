The name Lidiya (often spelled Lydia) is a deeply meaningful feminine name that traces its origins back to an ancient Iron Age kingdom in western Anatolia (modern-day Turkey). Historically renowned for its vast wealth and for introducing the world's very first minted coinage, the region gave rise to a name that literally translates to "woman from Lydia" or "noble one".
